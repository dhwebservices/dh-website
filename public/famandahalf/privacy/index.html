Privacy policy

Fam & a Half lets you share your location with people you choose. This page explains what we collect, who sees it, how long we keep it, and how to delete it. Last updated 6 October 2026.

The short version.
  • Your location is only shown to the people in your circles, and to anyone you send a time-limited share link to.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising or analytics tools.
  • You can pause sharing, change what each circle sees, export your data or delete your account from inside the app at any time.

Our lawful bases under UK GDPR are performing our contract with you (running the service you signed up for), your consent for optional features such as location history and your phone number, and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working.

Location in the background

If you allow "Always" location, Fam & a Half collects your location while the app is closed so your circles see where you are. It stops whenever you pause sharing or set a circle to "Off". Crash detection uses your phone's motion sensors on the phone itself while you're driving; sensor data is not uploaded.

Who can see your data

  • People in your circles see your location at the level you choose for that circle: precise, approximate (about 1 km), or off. The "Who can see me" screen lists everyone who can see you.
  • Anyone you send a share link to can see your live location until the link expires or you cancel it.
  • During an SOS, everyone in your circles sees your exact location, even if you had paused sharing, until you end it.

We use these companies to run the service. They only process data on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and photo storage, hosted in London, UK.
  • Cloudflare: delivers notifications to Apple.
  • Apple: push notifications, and maps and address lookups shown in the app.
  • Resend: sends your sign-in code emails.
  • what3words: only if you tap “Show what3words address” on someone, their coordinates are sent to what3words (opening their website or app) to look it up.

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We would only disclose it if the law requires us to.

Your choices and rights

  • Pause or change sharing at any time in Settings, per circle or everywhere.
  • Export a copy of your data from Settings.
  • Delete your account from Settings. Your account, location, history, journeys, places you added, photos and phone number are deleted straight away. Audit records are kept with your identity removed.

Under UK GDPR you can also ask us to access, correct, restrict or move your data, or object to how we use it. Email us and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

You must be 13 or over to create a Fam & a Half account. If you're a parent and believe your child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to people in a circle with you, and every request is checked on our servers, not just in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we design Fam & a Half to collect as little as it needs.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
