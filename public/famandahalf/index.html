Everything a family needs. Nothing it doesn't.

The features other apps charge for are free here, and some you won't find anywhere else.

🗺️

Live map

See everyone at once, with battery, and whether they're walking, cycling or driving.

🏠

Arrival alerts

Know when people get home, to school or to work, and get told if someone hasn't arrived by a set time.

🆘

SOS and crash detection

Hold SOS for 2 seconds and your circle sees your exact spot. Crash detection is free.

🚗

Can someone get me?

One tap asks your circle for a lift with your location. Everyone sees who's coming.

🎉

Night-out circles

A circle for friends on a night out or at a festival that deletes itself when it's over.

🫧

Bubble and privacy

Show only your rough area for a few hours, pause any time, and see who's checked on you.

Why “Fam & a Half”?

It's the name of our own family group chat: the family, plus the partners, friends and everyone else who's family really. We built the app we wanted for them, and made it free for everyone else too.
