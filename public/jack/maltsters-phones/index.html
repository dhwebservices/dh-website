Setting up Maltsters staff phones

How to get Shaun and any Maltsters staff onto their work email on their own phones, without the DH rules we put on our own devices. Their phones stay theirs: nothing locked down, no passcode rules, no wiping, and we can't see anything on them.

How it works, in one line

Our DH rules only reach a phone if that phone is signed up to Intune (Microsoft calls this "enrolling" or "managing" the device). Maltsters phones never get signed up. They only get the Outlook app with their work account, so none of our rules ever touch them.

Before you start

Never do these on a Maltsters phone

  • Install Intune Company Portal.
  • Go into iPhone Settings → General → VPN & Device Management and add a work account.
  • On Android, tap "Set up work profile" or "Register device" if anything ever asks.
  • Add a Maltsters person to any DHWS group.
  • Type someone's password for them. They type it themselves.

If the phone asks to "let your organisation manage this device", stop and press Cancel, then tell David. That's the step that would bring our rules onto their phone.

Who does which bit

Part 1 is David's. It's a one-off check in Intune that our DH rules can't reach Maltsters people. You don't have access to Intune, so he does it, with you reading this page out to him.

Part 2 is yours, in the pub, for each person's phone.

New Maltsters email accounts come from David, because they need a licence. Ask him first, and only then set up the phone.

Part 1: make sure our rules can't reach them (one-off)

David does this once on his login. It takes about 10 minutes.

  1. David

    Stop them signing up to Intune by mistake. Go to Intune → Devices → Enrollment → Device platform restrictions.

    intune.microsoft.com → Devices → Enrollment

    Create a restriction for iOS/iPadOS and one for Android. Set Personally owned devices to Block, and assign both to Client - The Maltsters Arms. If a Maltsters phone ever tries to sign up, it's refused.

  2. David

    Check Outlook app rules. Go to Intune → Apps → Protection. If any policy there is assigned to All users, add Client - The Maltsters Arms under Excluded groups. These are the only rules that can reach the Outlook app without Intune sign-up.

    intune.microsoft.com → Apps → Protection
  3. David

    Check phone rules. Go to Intune → Devices → Compliance, and then Configuration. For every iOS or Android policy (including IOS/IPADOS CONFIG), check it's assigned to DHWS groups and not to All users or All devices. If it's on All users, exclude Client - The Maltsters Arms.

  4. David

    Check sign-in rules. Go to Entra → Conditional Access. On DHWS - Staff require compliant device, add Client - The Maltsters Arms under Exclude, so it can never ask a Maltsters phone to be "compliant". Leave Block legacy authentication on for them, because it's harmless and protects their accounts.

    entra.microsoft.com → Conditional Access
  5. You

    Check every Maltsters person is in Client - The Maltsters Arms and in no DHWS groups. In admin.microsoft.com, open Users → Active users → their name → Groups. You can do this one yourself.

    admin.microsoft.com → Users

Part 2: set up each phone (in the pub)

About 10 minutes per person. They hold the phone and type their own password. You talk them through it.

  1. You

    Have them install two free apps from the App Store or Google Play:

    • Microsoft Outlook
    • Microsoft Authenticator

    Nothing else. Not Company Portal.

  2. You

    Open Outlook → Add account. They type their work email (for example shaun@malstersponty.com) and then their own password.

  3. You

    If Microsoft says "More information required", that's the sign-in security setup and it's fine. Choose the Authenticator app and follow the steps. It flips over to Authenticator and back.

  4. You

    If anything says "Allow my organisation to manage this device", "Register" or "Install Company Portal", press Cancel / No and tell David. Our setup shouldn't ever ask them that.

  5. You

    Check it works: they send you a test email and you reply. Their inbox should load in the Outlook app.

  6. You

    Show them the Maltsters branding at outlook.office.com in their phone's browser (the black bar with the pub logo). The Outlook app itself stays the normal Microsoft colours, and that's expected.

  7. You

    Write down who you set up and the date, and add it to David's end-of-day summary.

If something's wrong

"You can't get there from here" or "Your device must be managed": one of our DH sign-in rules is catching them. Stop. David needs to finish Part 1, step 4.

Outlook asks them to install Company Portal or Intune: an Outlook app rule is catching them. Press Cancel. David needs to finish Part 1, step 2.

They forgot their password: David or you can reset it in admin.microsoft.com. Give them the temporary one in person, never by text.

They lose their phone or leave the pub: tell David. He signs them out of everything and blocks the account. There's nothing to wipe on the phone, because we never managed it.
