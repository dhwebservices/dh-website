Maltsters branding in Microsoft 365

Shaun's email (shaun@malstersponty.com) lives on our Microsoft network. This guide gives him the Maltsters logo and colours across the top of Outlook and Microsoft 365, while everyone at DH keeps the DH look. Allow about 30 minutes, plus a few hours for Microsoft to catch up.

What Shaun will see

Every time he opens Outlook on the web, office.com or another Microsoft 365 web app, this bar is across the top:

This is a drawing to show the idea. The real thing will look slightly different.

The one thing we can't brand

The Microsoft sign-in page (where Shaun types his email and password) has one look for the whole network, so Shaun can't get his own version. He'll see the same sign-in page as you and David. As soon as he's signed in, he gets the Maltsters bar.

Don't try to change the sign-in page for him. That's under Entra → Custom branding, and any change there changes it for everyone at DH too.

Before you start

Don't touch these

  • "Default theme" in Custom themes. That's the DH look for everyone. Only ever add a new theme.
  • Entra → Custom branding (the sign-in page). It changes for everyone.
  • Never ask anyone for a password, and never type David's.

Who does which bit

Part 1 (make the group): you can do it, because your User Admin role covers groups.

Part 2 (make the theme): Microsoft only lets a Global Administrator do this, and you don't have that role any more. Get to the end of Part 1, then sit with David while he does Part 2 on his own login, reading this page out to him. It takes him 5 minutes.

Part 1: make a group for the Maltsters

Why a new group? Themes only work with a Microsoft 365 group. The existing "Client - The Maltsters Arms" group is a security group, which is why the Save button stayed grey when we tried before. Leave that group as it is. You're making a second one just for the branding.

  1. You

    On the computer, open the Microsoft 365 admin centre and sign in with your own DH account:

    admin.microsoft.com → Teams & groups

    If the menu on the left is short, press Show all at the bottom first. Then go to Teams & groups → Active teams & groups.

  2. You

    Make sure the Teams & Microsoft 365 groups tab is selected along the top, then press Add a Microsoft 365 group.

  3. You

    Basics: paste the group name and description from above. Press Next.

  4. You

    Owners: add yourself and David. Press Next.

  5. You

    Members: add Shaun Shah (shaun@malstersponty.com) and nobody else. Press Next.

  6. You

    Settings:

    • Group email address: paste maltsters-theme and leave the domain as dhwebsiteservices.co.uk.
    • Privacy: Private.
    • Untick "Add Microsoft Teams to your group". He doesn't need a Team.

    Press Next, check it all, then Create group.

  7. You

    Wait about 15 minutes for the group to finish setting up. Then search for Maltsters Arms Theme in Active teams & groups and check that Shaun is the only member.

Part 2: make the Maltsters theme

David does this on his login, with you reading out the steps.

  1. David

    Open Organisation profile:

    admin.microsoft.com → Settings → Org settings → Organization profile

    Press Custom themes.

  2. David

    Press Add theme. Don't open "Default theme", because that one is the DH look for everyone.

  3. David

    General tab:

    • Name: The Maltsters Arms
    • Groups: type Maltsters and choose Maltsters Arms Theme
    • Tick Prevent users from overriding their theme
    • Tick Show the user's display name
  4. David

    Logos tab: paste the logo address into Default logo and Alternate logo, then paste the on-click link.

  5. David

    Colors tab: paste the three colour codes:

    • Navigation bar: #09080A
    • Text and icon: #FFFFFF
    • Accent: #C8102E

    If it warns about colour contrast, that's only advice. You can still save.

  6. David

    Press Save. If Save stays grey, the group picked in step 3 is probably the old security group. Pick Maltsters Arms Theme instead.

Part 3: check it works

  1. You

    Wait. It can take a few hours, sometimes until the next day, before the theme reaches Shaun.

  2. You

    To see it for yourself: add yourself to the Maltsters Arms Theme group. Then open a private browser window, sign in at office.com, and check the bar is black with the Maltsters logo.

  3. You

    Take yourself back out of the group as soon as you've checked, or you'll keep the Maltsters look. Someone in two theme groups gets the plain default instead.

  4. You

    Next time you're with Shaun, ask him to open Outlook at outlook.office.com and check he sees the Maltsters bar. Help him save it to his phone's home screen.

  5. You

    Tick "Maltsters Microsoft branding" on today's list.

If something's wrong

Save is grey: the theme is pointed at a security group. It has to be the Microsoft 365 group from Part 1.

No logo, just the colours: open the logo address in a browser. If it doesn't load, tell Claude. On narrow screens, Microsoft sometimes hides the logo to make room, and that's normal.

Everyone at DH has the Maltsters look: someone edited "Default theme". Tell David straight away.

Shaun still sees the normal look after a day: check he's in Maltsters Arms Theme, and that he isn't in any other theme group.
