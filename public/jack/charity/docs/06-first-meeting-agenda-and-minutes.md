# Valleys Digital: Meeting Papers

A CIO only exists once the Charity Commission registers it, so there are two meetings:

- **Meeting A: founders' meeting**, held **before** we apply. We agree the constitution and who the first trustees are, and sign the declarations.
- **Meeting B: first trustees' meeting**, held **after** we're registered. We appoint officers, adopt the policies, open the bank account and agree the DH arrangement.

The bank will want the signed minutes of Meeting B.
