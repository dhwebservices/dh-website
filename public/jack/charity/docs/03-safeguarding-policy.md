# Safeguarding Policy — Valleys Digital

DRAFT for the trustees to adopt at the first meeting.

## Scope
This policy covers:
- our free digital help drop-ins, which may include older people and adults at risk;
- training sessions; and
- any contact with children or young people through the groups we work with.

It applies to:
- trustees;
- volunteers; and
- anyone working for us, including contractors.

## Our commitments
- Everyone has the right to be safe from abuse, neglect and exploitation, including financial and online abuse and scams.
- We take every concern seriously and act on it.
- We follow the **Wales Safeguarding Procedures** (safeguarding.wales) and work with Rhondda Cynon Taf's safeguarding services.

## Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL)
The trustees appoint a DSL, and a deputy, from among the trustees. The DSL:
- is the first contact for any concern;
- decides whether to make a referral;
- keeps records secure; and
- makes sure training happens.

## Safe practice at drop-ins and sessions
- Sessions take place in public venues (libraries, community centres), never in someone's home.
- At least two people from Valleys Digital are present at every drop-in.
- **We never ask for, look at, type in or write down anyone's passwords, PINs, bank details or one-time codes.** We show people how to enter them themselves.
- We never take control of a person's money or accounts, and we never accept gifts beyond something trivial.
- No one is left alone with a child, and children attend only with a parent or carer.
- Anyone running sessions regularly gets a **basic DBS check** before they start. If the work changes so that it counts as regulated activity, they get the right level of check for it.
- Everyone running sessions does safeguarding awareness training before their first session. Free e-learning is available through the Wales Safeguarding Procedures and Interlink RCT.

## If you are worried about someone
1. **If someone is in immediate danger, call 999.**
2. Listen. Don't promise to keep it secret. Don't investigate or question.
3. Write down what you saw or were told, in the person's words, with the date and time. Do this the same day.
4. Tell the DSL the same day. If you can't reach them, tell the deputy.
5. The DSL decides whether to refer the concern to the local authority:
   - Rhondda Cynon Taf adult or children's services; or
   - for a scam or fraud, Action Fraud (0300 123 2040).

   The DSL records the decision either way.
6. If the concern is about the DSL, tell another trustee.

## Records
Safeguarding records are kept securely, shared only with people who need to know, and handled under our data protection policy.

## Review
The trustees review this policy every year, and after any serious incident.
