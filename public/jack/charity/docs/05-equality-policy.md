# Equality, Diversity and Inclusion Policy — Valleys Digital

DRAFT for the trustees to adopt at the first meeting.

Valleys Digital exists to help people and groups who are left behind online, so being open to everyone is central to what we do.

1. We welcome everyone, including as members, volunteers, trustees and the groups we help. We don't treat anyone unfairly because of any protected characteristic under the Equality Act 2010:
   - age
   - disability
   - gender reassignment
   - marriage and civil partnership
   - pregnancy and maternity
   - race
   - religion or belief
   - sex
   - sexual orientation

   We also don't treat anyone unfairly because of their income, background or how confident they are with technology.
2. **Welsh language.** We offer our services in Welsh and English wherever we can. Welsh-language groups are welcome, and we can build bilingual websites. We follow the spirit of the Welsh Language (Wales) Measure 2011.
3. **Accessibility:**
   - Websites we fund are built to WCAG 2.2 AA, so they work with screen readers and keyboards and on phones.
   - Drop-ins are held in venues with step-free access where possible.
   - We provide large-print and plain-English materials.
4. **Fair selection.** When there are more applications for free websites than we can fund, we choose using published criteria. We give priority to groups that serve people facing disadvantage.
5. **Monitoring.** With people's consent, we collect anonymous information about who we reach. We use it to find gaps and report to funders.
6. **Complaints.** Anyone who feels they were treated unfairly can contact any trustee. The trustees look into it and reply within 28 days.

The trustees review this policy every year.
