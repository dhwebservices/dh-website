# Data Protection Policy and Privacy Notice — Valleys Digital

DRAFT for the trustees to adopt at the first meeting.

## Part 1: Policy (for trustees and volunteers)

Valleys Digital is the data controller for the personal information it holds. We follow the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

**What we hold:**
- names and contact details of people at groups that apply for, or get, a website;
- members' and volunteers' details;
- drop-in attendance (first name only, unless more is needed);
- donor and sponsor details; and
- safeguarding records.

**Rules:**
1. Collect only what we need, for a clear reason.
2. Store it only in the charity's own accounts, never on personal devices or in personal email. Use two-factor sign-in on every account.
3. Share it only when needed. Never sell it or use it for anyone else's marketing. Details of groups are not passed to DH Website Services for sales: DH gets only what it needs to build a site the charity has approved.
4. Delete it when it's no longer needed. Our retention periods:
   - applications that don't go ahead: 12 months;
   - grant records: as long as the funder requires (often 7 years);
   - safeguarding records: as advised by the DSL.
5. **Breaches.** Tell the secretary at once. The trustees decide within 72 hours whether to report it to the ICO.
6. **Requests.** Anyone can ask for a copy of their data, or ask for it to be corrected or deleted. We reply within one month.
7. **ICO fee.** The secretary completes the ICO's online self-assessment ("Do I need to pay a fee?") and records the result. Whatever the result, we still follow these rules.

The secretary is responsible for data protection.

## Part 2: Privacy notice (to publish on our website and on sign-up forms)

**Who we are:** Valleys Digital, a charitable incorporated organisation (CIO) in Rhondda Cynon Taf, registered charity number ______. Contact: ____________ (email).

**What we collect and why:**
- **Your group's name, your name, email, phone and details about your group.** We use these to assess and deliver a free website or digital help. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, or steps before an agreement.
- **With your permission:** a quote from you in our funding bids and reports, to show funders the need. You can withdraw permission at any time. Lawful basis: consent.
- **Donor details:** to process donations and to claim Gift Aid. Lawful basis: legal obligation or legitimate interests.

**Who we share it with:**
- DH Website Services, which builds and hosts the websites. It receives only the details needed for your site.
- Funders, in summary form, or with your consent.
- Our email, form and hosting providers.

We never sell your data.

**How long we keep it:** see the policy above, or ask us.

**Your rights:** you can ask for access to your data, or for it to be corrected or deleted. You can also object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).
