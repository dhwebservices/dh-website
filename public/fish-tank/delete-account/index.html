Delete your account and data

This page explains how to delete your The Fish Tank account, published by DH Website Services, and exactly what happens to your data when you do.

How to request deletion

  1. Open The Fish Tank and note the username you play under. It is shown on the leaderboard and in the multiplayer lobby.
  2. Email support@dhwebsiteservices.co.uk from any address, with the subject Delete my Fish Tank account, and include that username.
  3. We delete the account and reply to confirm, normally within 7 days and always within 30 days.
Email us to delete your account

You do not need an account to play. The single-player game works fully offline, and deleting your account does not remove the game or your offline progress from your device.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your username and account identifierDeleted permanently. The username becomes available for anyone to use again.
Device identifier used to keep your account on your deviceDeleted permanently.
Notification token for your phoneDeleted permanently. No further notifications are sent.
Scores, progress and leaderboard entriesDeleted permanently and removed from all leaderboards.
Friends and friend requestsDeleted permanently.
Gifts and rewards sent to youDeleted permanently, whether claimed or not.

What is kept, and for how long

Anonymous crash reports are kept for up to 90 days so that we can fix faults in the game. They contain the fault message, the app version and the device platform. Once your account is deleted, they are no longer linked to you or to your username.

Nothing else is retained, and none of it is sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

If you would rather keep playing but remove a particular thing — for example your leaderboard score, or your notification token so we can no longer send you notifications — email the same address and say which. You can also turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.
