# Email templates

Merge fields: `{{business}}`, `{{first_name}}`, `{{trade}}`, `{{observation}}`.

`{{observation}}` is the one specific, true thing you noticed — the reason this
is not a mass mailshot. Examples:

- "you're taking bookings through Fresha but there's no site of your own"
- "your Facebook page is doing the work a website normally would"
- "your listing shows a phone number but no opening hours"

If you cannot write a true `{{observation}}` for a prospect, do not email them.
That sentence is the difference between outreach and spam.

## 3. Phone script — for sole traders

Most of this list is sole traders, who cannot lawfully be cold-emailed
(`compliance.md`). Phone is the route, and for a 7-day offer it is the faster
one anyway.

> "Morning — is that {{first_name}}? My name's David, I run DH Website Services
> up on Coedpenmaen Road. I'll be thirty seconds.
>
> I noticed {{business}} hasn't got a website — {{observation}}. We're doing
> half price this week: five-page site, live in seven days, hosting and the
> domain included, £224.50 instead of £449.
>
> Is that something worth a fifteen-minute chat, or shall I leave you be?"

If yes → book it into the widget while on the call. If no → thank them, mark
`no` in the CSV, do not call again.
