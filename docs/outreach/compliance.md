# Who we may email

UK cold B2B email is governed by PECR reg. 22 and UK GDPR. The rules are not
onerous, but they do rule out part of the "every small business" target.

## The corporate / individual split

PECR treats email marketing differently depending on the subscriber:

- **Corporate subscribers** — limited companies (Ltd), LLPs, PLCs, public
  bodies. Unsolicited marketing email is **permitted** without prior consent,
  provided we identify ourselves and offer an opt-out.
- **Individual subscribers** — **sole traders and unincorporated
  partnerships**. These are treated like consumers. Unsolicited marketing email
  needs **prior consent** or the soft opt-in (an existing customer relationship).

This matters here: a large share of small Pontypridd businesses with no website
are sole traders. **Emailing them cold is not lawful.** Reach them another way —
phone, post, in person, or a Google Business Profile message. Post and phone
(to a number not on the CTPS) are not restricted by PECR reg. 22.

So: check Companies House before adding anyone to the email list. If they are
not a registered company or LLP, they go on the call/post list, not this one.

## Requirements for every email we do send

1. **Identify the sender.** DH Website Services, with a real postal address and
   a working reply address. No disguised or concealed identity.
2. **Working opt-out** in every message, honoured immediately and permanently.
3. **Accurate subject line.** No fake "Re:" or "following up on our call".
4. **Suppression list.** Anyone who opts out, or does not reply after the final
   follow-up, is never contacted again.
5. **Records.** Keep the source of each address and the date obtained. This is
   the lawful-basis paper trail if the ICO ever asks.

## Addresses

Only use addresses **published by the business itself** for enquiries — their
Google Business Profile, their Facebook page, a directory listing they created.

Do not use:
- Guessed or pattern-built addresses (`info@`, `hello@` invented from a name).
  These bounce, and bounces damage domain reputation.
- Scraped personal addresses of named individuals.
- Addresses from purchased lists — the consent never transfers cleanly.

## Volume

Cold outreach from the same domain that sends client mail is a real risk. Keep
to ~20–30/day, watch the bounce rate, and stop if it climbs above ~3%.

## Opt-out line

Use this verbatim at the foot of every outreach email:

> If you'd rather not hear from us again, just reply "unsubscribe" and we'll
> remove you from our list straight away.
>
> DH Website Services, 36b Coedpenmaen Road, Pontypridd, CF37 4LP
